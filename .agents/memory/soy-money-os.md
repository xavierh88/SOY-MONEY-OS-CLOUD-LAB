---
name: SOY MONEY OS evidence gates
description: Durable product rules for the opportunity pipeline and evidence semantics.
---

The V1 must treat local research and seeded records as TEST_SIMULATION, never as demand proof. Only REAL_VERIFIED evidence can unlock later gates; human approval remains required for sensitive transitions.

**Why:** The product's core trust boundary is distinguishing hypotheses from verified demand and preventing autonomous financial actions.

**How to apply:** Preserve explicit proof statuses, source traceability, blocked gates, timeouts/retries, and human checkpoints when adding future engines or integrations.
